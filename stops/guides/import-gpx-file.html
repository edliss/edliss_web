How to import a GPX file into Stops

Most hiking, cycling and GPS apps can export GPX, and so can Stops. The waypoints in a GPX file become stops, with their names, notes and dates.

  • Free
  • About 2 minutes
  • iPhone and iPad

What you need

  • A GPX file saved in the Files app, in iCloud Drive or on your iPhone.

Step by step

  1. Save the GPX file to Files

    Export the GPX from the app or website it comes from and save it to Files. Waypoints are what Stops imports; a file that only has a route brings in the route’s points instead.

  2. Open Import in Stops

    Open Journal from the book button at the end of the tab bar, then tap the Settings gear. Choose Import, then GPX File, and pick the file.

  3. Check the places and import them

    The list is named after the file. Untick anything you don’t want and tap Import.

Good to know

  • A waypoint’s type becomes its category when it matches one of Stops’ categories, such as Viewpoint, Camping or Waterfall. A GPX exported from Stops comes back exactly as it left.
  • Recorded tracks are left out: Stops keeps places, not paths.
  • Stops exports KML for Google Earth and My Maps, but imports GPX only.
