French Numbers

French Numbers Listening Practice

Focused listening practice for spoken French numbers in everyday situations. French Numbers is iPhone-only for now.

One thing I underestimated after moving to France was how difficult spoken numbers can be in everyday life.

Grammar and vocabulary are one thing. But hearing someone quickly say a price at a market, a phone number over the phone, or a time in conversation is a completely different kind of challenge.

French Numbers was built specifically to practice that skill.

Instead of trying to become a complete language-learning platform, the app focuses on a narrow but surprisingly important problem: understanding spoken French numbers confidently in real-world situations.

The app includes several focused practice modes:

  • basic numbers
  • phone numbers
  • money and prices
  • telling time in French

Users can practice different number ranges independently, including the ranges many learners struggle with most, especially numbers above 69.

The app also supports multiple French dialects and lets users adjust speaking speed to match their comfort level.

I increasingly prefer software that focuses deeply on one useful task instead of trying to cover everything at once. French Numbers was designed with that philosophy in mind.

The goal is simple: hear French numbers more often, practice regularly, and slowly reach the point where they stop feeling stressful in daily life.

French Numbers is an iPhone app for focused listening drills, with simple progress stats to help regular practice feel visible. Learn more about French Numbers.
